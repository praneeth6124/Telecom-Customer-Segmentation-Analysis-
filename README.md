# 📊 Telecom Customer Segmentation Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that transforms raw telecom customer data into actionable business insights using Python, SQL-style analytical thinking, Machine Learning, and Power BI.

The project focuses on understanding customer behavior, identifying high-risk customer segments, quantifying revenue exposure, and presenting insights through an interactive executive dashboard.

---

# 📌 Business Problem

Telecom companies lose significant revenue due to customer churn.

Instead of treating every customer equally, businesses need to understand:

- Which customer groups are most valuable?
- Which customers are at the highest risk of leaving?
- What characteristics define each customer segment?
- Where should retention efforts be focused?
- How much revenue is potentially at risk?

This project answers those questions using a complete analytics workflow.

# 📊 Analysis Performed

## Data Cleaning

- Removed unnecessary columns
- Checked missing values
- Encoded categorical variables
- Prepared features for analysis

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyzed customer characteristics including:

- Contract Type
- Internet Service
- Monthly Charges
- Total Charges
- Customer Tenure
- Payment Method

Identified important behavioral patterns before segmentation.

---

## Feature Engineering

Created analytical features to improve customer profiling and business interpretation.

---

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into behavioral segments using K-Means clustering.

Each segment was analyzed based on:

- Customer Count
- Average Monthly Charges
- Average Total Charges
- Average Tenure
- Churn Rate
- Revenue Contribution

---

## Anomaly Detection

Isolation Forest was used to identify customers with unusual behavioral patterns that may require business attention.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Analyzed over **7,000 telecom customers**.
- Identified **two distinct customer segments**.
- High-value customers also exhibit the highest churn risk.
- Estimated monthly revenue exposure exceeds **$135K**.
- Anomaly detection identified customers requiring further investigation.

---

# 📋 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention campaigns for high-value customers.
- Encourage Month-to-Month customers to move toward long-term contracts.
- Design personalized offers for high-risk customer segments.
- Monitor anomaly-flagged customers for early intervention.
